Important before installing

Clear the CEP cache

For the update to work correctly, delete the Adobe CEP cache folder before opening Premiere or After Effects.

🍎 macOS

1. Close Premiere and After Effects

2. In Finder press ⌘ + Shift + G and paste:

~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/

3. Delete the cep_caches folder

4. Reinstall by copying the folder to /Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions

🪟 Windows

1. Close Premiere and After Effects

2. Press Win + R and paste:

%AppData%\Adobe\CEP\

3. Delete the cep_caches folder

4. Reinstall by copying the folder to C:\Program Files (x86)\Common Files\Adobe\CEP\extensions
